GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM & NATURAL GAS
SCHEME: PRADHAN MANTRI UJJWALA YOJANA (PMUY)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://pmuy.gov.in | HELPLINE: 1967 / 1800-266-6696

1. OBJECTIVE:
Provides clean cooking fuel (LPG connections) to adult women from poor and Below Poverty Line (BPL) households, replacing hazardous biomass fuels (firewood, coal, cow dung).

2. ASSISTANCE & PERKS:
- Free LPG connection with ₹1,600 government financial assistance covering cylinder security deposit, regulator, safety hose, and installation.
- First refill and LPG stove provided on interest-free loan basis / subsidized basis.
- Targeted subsidy on domestic cylinder refills credited directly to bank account.

3. ELIGIBILITY:
- Adult woman (18+ years) from BPL family, SC/ST, PMAY (Gramin), Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY), Most Backward Classes (MBC), or SECC-2011 deprivation list.
- No existing LPG connection in the household.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Aadhaar card of applicant woman and adult family members.
- BPL ration card or SECC status certificate.
- Bank passbook with IFSC code seeded with Aadhaar.
- Passport photo.

5. MIDDLEMEN WARNING:
Connection applications are processed directly by authorized LPG distributors (Indane, Bharat Gas, HP Gas) and CSCs. Do NOT pay unauthorized intermediaries.
