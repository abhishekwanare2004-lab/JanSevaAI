GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF FINANCE
SCHEME: PRADHAN MANTRI JEEVAN JYOTI BIMA YOJANA (PMJJBY)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://jansuraksha.gov.in

1. OVERVIEW & BENEFIT:
PMJJBY is a pure term life insurance scheme offering ₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh) life cover in case of death due to any reason (natural or accidental).
- Premium: ₹436 per annum per member.
- Coverage Period: 1st June to 31st May. Auto-renewable annually up to age 55.

2. ELIGIBILITY:
- Any person aged 18 to 50 years having an active savings bank account.
- Must link Aadhaar to bank account.
- A simple self-declaration of good health is accepted.

3. CLAIM SETTLEMENT:
- Nominee submits death certificate, claim form, and KYC at the branch where the account was held.
- Benefit of ₹2 Lakh is transferred directly to the nominee's account via DBT.
