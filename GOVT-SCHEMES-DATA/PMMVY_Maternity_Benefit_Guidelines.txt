GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF WOMEN & CHILD DEVELOPMENT
SCHEME: PRADHAN MANTRI MATRU VANDANA YOJANA (PMMVY)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://pmmvy.nic.in / https://wcd.nic.in

1. OBJECTIVE & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:
PMMVY provides partial wage compensation and cash incentives of ₹5,000 to pregnant women and lactating mothers for health, rest, and nutrition during their first living child.
The incentive is paid in three installments via DBT:
- 1st Installment (₹1,000): Early registration of pregnancy at Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or health facility.
- 2nd Installment (₹2,000): After at least one antenatal check-up (ANC) after 6 months of pregnancy.
- 3rd Installment (₹2,000): Child birth registration and first cycle of vaccinations (BCG, OPV, DPT, Hepatitis B).
*Combined with Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY), total maternity benefit reaches ₹6,000.

2. ELIGIBILITY:
- Pregnant women aged 19 years and above for the first living child.
- Regular employees of Central/State government or PSUs are not eligible.
- Pregnant students who meet age criteria (19+) are fully eligible.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Aadhaar Card of beneficiary (mandatory)
- Mother and Child Protection (MCP) Card
- Bank/Post Office passbook seeded with Aadhaar
- Proof of age and identity.

4. MITIGATING MIDDLEMEN:
Application Form 1-A is available FREE of charge at all Anganwadi Centres and ASHA workers. No fee is required. Avoid any agent asking for money to release DBT installments.
