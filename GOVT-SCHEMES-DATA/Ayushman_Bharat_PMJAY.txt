GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - NATIONAL HEALTH AUTHORITY (NHA)
SCHEME: AYUSHMAN BHARAT - PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (AB-PMJAY)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://pmjay.gov.in | TOLL-FREE HELPLINE: 14555 / 1800-111-565

1. SCOPE & COVERAGE:
- World's largest government-funded healthcare assurance scheme.
- Health cover of ₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakh) per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalization.
- Completely cashless and paperless at all empaneled public and private hospitals across India.
- Covers pre-existing conditions from Day 1 with no waiting period.
- Includes pre-hospitalization (3 days) and post-hospitalization (15 days) care and medicines.

2. ELIGIBILITY:
- Identified based on rural and urban deprivation criteria under SECC 2011 (e.g. landless laborers, kuccha houses, female-headed households, manual scavengers).
- No restriction on family size, age, or gender.

3. HOW TO AVAIL:
- Check eligibility on pmjay.gov.in or call 14555.
- Obtain Ayushman Golden Card at any empaneled hospital helpdesk (Ayushman Mitra) or CSC.
- Free treatment: Never pay cash to any broker or hospital agent.
