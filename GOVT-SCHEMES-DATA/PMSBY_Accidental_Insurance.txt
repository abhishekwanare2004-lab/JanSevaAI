GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF FINANCE (DEPARTMENT OF FINANCIAL SERVICES)
SCHEME: PRADHAN MANTRI SURAKSHA BIMA YOJANA (PMSBY)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://jansuraksha.gov.in | HELPLINE: 1800-180-1111 / 1800-110-001

1. PURPOSE & COVERAGE:
PMSBY provides affordable one-year accidental death and disability cover:
- Accidental Death: ₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh)
- Total and irrecoverable loss of both eyes or loss of use of both hands/feet: ₹2,00,000
- Total and irrecoverable loss of sight of one eye or loss of use of one hand/foot: ₹1,00,000

2. PREMIUM & DURATION:
- Annual Premium: Only ₹20 per annum per member.
- Coverage Period: 1st June to 31st May each year.
- Premium is automatically debited from the subscriber's bank account before 31st May each year.

3. ELIGIBILITY:
- All individual bank account holders aged 18 to 70 years.
- Indian citizens with an active savings account linked to Aadhaar.
- No medical examination or income certificate required.

4. EXCLUSIONS:
- Natural death (due to disease, heart attack, or illness) is NOT covered.
- Suicide or intentional self-injury, drug/alcohol-influenced accidents, war/crime casualties.

5. DIRECT ACCESS & ANTI-BROKER ADVICE:
Enrollment is done directly through your bank branch, NetBanking, or mobile banking app. Never pay extra cash to middlemen; the annual ₹20 premium is auto-dedited transparently.
