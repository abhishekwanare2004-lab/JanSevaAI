GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF RURAL DEVELOPMENT
SCHEME: MAHATMA GANDHI NATIONAL RURAL EMPLOYMENT GUARANTEE ACT (MGNREGA)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://nrega.nic.in

1. LEGAL GUARANTEE:
- Provides legal entitlement of at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment per financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.
- If employment is not provided within 15 days of application, an unemployment allowance is legally mandated.

2. WORK PERMITTED:
- Water conservation, watershed management, afforestation, drought proofing, rural connectivity, flood control, sanitation (toilets), and individual beneficiary works (wells, cattle sheds).
- Material to labor ratio of 40:60 strictly maintained.

3. WAGE PAYMENT:
- Daily wages notified by government are directly deposited to the bank/post office account of the worker.
- Equal wages for men and women with no discrimination.

4. ANTI-EXPLOITATION SAFEGUARD:
- Job Cards are issued FREE of cost by the Gram Panchayat within 15 days of applying.
- Beware of middlemen trying to withhold Job Cards or demanding bribes. Report directly to Gram Pradhan, BDO, or ombudsman.
