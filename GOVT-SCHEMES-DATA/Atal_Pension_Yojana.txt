GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - PENSION FUND REGULATORY AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (PFRDA)
SCHEME: ATAL PENSION YOJANA (APY)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://npscra.nsdl.co.in | https://atalpensionnps.in

1. CORE PURPOSE:
Provides a guaranteed social security pension for unorganized sector workers (maids, drivers, farmers, artisans, shop assistants).
- Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000 from age 60 until death.
- On subscriber's demise, exact same pension continues to spouse for lifetime.
- On demise of both subscriber and spouse, the entire accumulated pension wealth (corpus) is returned to the nominee.

2. ELIGIBILITY:
- Indian citizens aged 18 to 40 years.
- Active savings bank account with auto-debit facility.
- Non-income tax payer.
