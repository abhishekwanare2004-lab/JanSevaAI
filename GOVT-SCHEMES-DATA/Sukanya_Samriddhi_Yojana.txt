GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF FINANCE & WCD
SCHEME: SUKANYA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA (BETI BACHAO BETI PADHAO CAMPAIGN)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://www.indiapost.gov.in

1. OVERVIEW:
Small deposit savings scheme for girl children offering the highest sovereign-backed interest rate with Triple Tax Exemption (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt under 80C).

2. ELIGIBILITY & DEPOSIT LIMITS:
- Can be opened by parents/guardians for a girl child from birth up to age 10.
- Minimum initial and annual deposit: ₹250.
- Maximum deposit: ₹1,50,000 per financial year.
- Deposits can be made for 15 years from account opening.
- Maturity period: 21 years from account opening.
- Partial withdrawal (up to 50%) permitted after age 18 for higher education.
