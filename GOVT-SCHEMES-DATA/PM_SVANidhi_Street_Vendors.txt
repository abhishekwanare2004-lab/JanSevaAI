GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF HOUSING & URBAN AFFAIRS
SCHEME: PM STREET VENDOR'S ATMANIRBHAR NIDHI (PM SVANIDHI)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in

1. WORKING CAPITAL CREDIT:
- 1st Tranche: Collateral-free working capital loan up to ₹10,000 (repayable in 12 months).
- 2nd Tranche: Enhanced loan up to ₹20,000 on timely repayment of 1st loan.
- 3rd Tranche: Enhanced loan up to ₹50,000 on timely repayment of 2nd loan.

2. INCENTIVES:
- 7% per annum interest subsidy credited directly to bank account on timely repayment.
- Cash-back up to ₹1,200 per year on eligible digital transactions (UPI).

3. ELIGIBILITY:
- Urban street vendors holding Certificate of Vending (CoV) / Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) or Letter of Recommendation (LoR).
- Completely free of collateral. No processing fee.
