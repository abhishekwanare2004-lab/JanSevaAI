PUBLIC CITIZEN ADVISORY: DEMYSTIFYING PUBLIC SERVICES & ELIMINATING EXPLOITATIVE MIDDLEMEN
PREPARED FOR: RURAL GOVERNANCE & COMMUNITY EMPOWERMENT
PROJECT GROUP: 82 | PCCOE DEPT OF CSE (AI & ML)

1. RECOGNIZE EXPLOITATIVE MIDDLEMEN:
Rural citizens and farmers frequently encounter unauthorized brokers ('dalals') who:
- Demand illegal commissions (₹500 - ₹2,000) for filling forms for PM-KISAN, PMMVY, or Job Cards.
- Misinform citizens about eligibility rules or invent fictitious 'approval fees'.
- Withhold original documents (Aadhaar, Job Cards, Bank Passbooks, Land 7/12 extracts).

2. OFFICIAL TRUTHS & TRANSPARENT RULES:
- Every welfare scheme registration (PM-KISAN, MGNREGA, PMUY, PMSBY, PMJJBY, Ayushman Bharat) is completely FREE or has nominal fixed statutory charges (e.g. ₹20/year for PMSBY auto-debited).
- All government subsidy and benefit disbursements occur strictly via DIRECT BENEFIT TRANSFER (DBT) directly into the verified bank account. No cash handovers occur.
- You can apply directly at your local Gram Panchayat office, Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) at Common Service Centre (CSC), or through official government portals.
