GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE & FARMERS WELFARE
SCHEME: PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN)
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://pmkisan.gov.in | HELPLINE: 155261 / 1800115526 / 011-24300606

1. OBJECTIVE & BENEFIT:
PM-KISAN provides income support of ₹6,000 per year to all landholding farmer families with cultivable land.
The financial assistance is disbursed in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each every four months:
- Period 1: April to July
- Period 2: August to November
- Period 3: December to March
All funds are transferred directly into the bank accounts of beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Small, marginal, and all cultivable landholding farmer families.
- Land record (Khatauni / Khasra / RoR) must be registered in the name of the farmer in state land records.
- Exclusion: Institutional landholders, constitutional post holders, retired/serving government employees, professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs), and income tax payers.

3. MANDATORY KYC & PROCEDURES:
- eKYC is mandatory via OTP on pmkisan.gov.in or biometric verification at Common Service Centres (CSC).
- Bank account must be seeded with Aadhaar and mapped with NPCI.
- Registration can be completed online via 'Farmers Corner' on pmkisan.gov.in or through village Patwari/Lekhpal.

4. EXPLOITATIVE MIDDLEMEN WARNING (दलालों से सावधान):
PM-KISAN registration is completely FREE on the government portal. Do NOT pay any commission or service charge to local brokers or intermediaries. In case of issues, contact the District Agriculture Officer or call the toll-free helpline 155261.
